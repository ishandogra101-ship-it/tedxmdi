● About the event

TEDxMDIGurgaon is a student-run stage where one big idea gets twelve minutes and a room that actually listens.

We run out of MDI Gurgaon as an independently organised TED event. Six editions in, the stage has held a Union Minister, a commercial airline captain who flew the world's first all-women polar flight, a choreographer, a soldier, and founders who built and lost and built again.

No panels that run long. No keynote that sells you something. Eight talks, a real Q&A after each one, and a theme that ties them together. Edition 7.0 lands on 17 January 2027.
